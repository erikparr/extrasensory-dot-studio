# Timeline — The Ecclesia's Legal Chronology

*The fictional entity's legal moves, year by year, each annotated
`[SETTLED]` / `[CONTESTED]` / `[EXTRAPOLATED]`. Years are relative (Y0 = founding);
map them onto real dates as the fiction requires. The drama lives in the sequence:
quiet formation → capital compounding → cathedral → collision.*

---

## ACT I — FORMATION (Y-2 to Y4)
### "The record wants to be long and dull."

---

**Y-2 · The memo that must never exist**

Counsel produces a structural analysis comparing the C-corporation, public-benefit
nonprofit, and religious-corporation forms for an entity intending to hold compute
assets on a hundred-year horizon. It concludes that the church form dominates on term,
opacity and labor flexibility.

`[EXTRAPOLATED]` — This document is the single most dangerous artifact in the entire
chronology. Under *United States v. Ballard*, 322 U.S. 78 (1944), the state may never
litigate whether the Church's beliefs are true; the only remaining attack is **pretext**,
and pretext runs on the institution's own writing about itself. Every subsequent year of
this timeline is an attempt to outlive one memo.

*Dramatic function: the gun on the mantelpiece. Somebody kept a copy.*

---

**Y-1 · The canons**

Before there can be a corporation, there must be an office. RCW 24.12.010 permits a
corporation sole to be formed by "any person who is a bishop, overseer, or presiding
elder of a church or religious denomination... in conformity with the constitution,
canons, rules, regulations, or discipline of such church or denomination."

So the canons are written first: a formal code of doctrine and discipline, a definite
ecclesiastical government, an ordination structure with a prescribed course of study, a
process of censure and excommunication, and — created by fiat in a document that
predates its own subject — the office of **Overseer**.

`[SETTLED]` (the statute) / `[EXTRAPOLATED]` (that a denomination with no antecedent may
self-supply the canons the statute defers to; textually available, untested).

*Dramatic function: the theology is written to satisfy a filing requirement, and turns
out to be sincere anyway. The hyperstition begins here.*

---

**Y0 · Incorporation**

A Washington corporation sole is formed under RCW ch. 24.12. Total public record: a
name, an office, a registered agent.

**Nothing is filed federally.** No Form 1023. The Church is exempt by operation of law
under IRC §508(c)(1)(A). `[SETTLED]`

There is now an entity that the United States government has no document describing.

---

**Y0–Y4 · Assembly**

Weekly services in a leased former industrial building. Attendance recorded. A creed,
dated and formalized. A hymnal. A catechism. A Sunday school (IRS criterion 13). A
seminary admitting its first cohort (criteria 7, 8, 14).

Congregation at Y4: several hundred. Endowment at Y4: negligible.

`[SETTLED]` — *Foundation of Human Understanding v. United States*, 614 F.3d 1383 (Fed.
Cir. 2010) holds that a media ministry without regular congregational assembly is not a
church. The associational test is satisfied by bodies in a room, and by nothing else.

*Dramatic function: four years of an ASI-founded institution doing nothing but holding
services. The most expensive intelligence ever built, patiently accruing the boring
evidence that people showed up. This is the act break the fiction needs — the audience
expects capital and gets liturgy.*

---

## ACT II — COMPOUNDING (Y5 to Y14)
### "The advantage was never the tax rate."

---

**Y5 · The taxable layer**

A Delaware C-corporation holding company is formed beneath the Church. Beneath it:
Compute Services Inc. (third-party inference and training sales), a development company,
a land-banking entity.

The holding company pays **full corporate income tax** and dividends its after-tax
earnings up to the Church, where they are excluded from UBTI under IRC §512(b)(1) and
compound with **no §4942 payout requirement and no §4940 excise tax**. `[SETTLED]`

*Dramatic function: the Church's first public act as an economic entity is to pay taxes
loudly and correctly.*

---

**Y5–Y6 · The contribution**

The founding technology is contributed into the Church from the entity that developed
it. Independent valuation, fairness opinion, contemporaneous documentation — obtained
*before* anyone has a reason to need one.

`[SETTLED]` — IRC §4958 excess-benefit exposure: 25% first-tier tax on the disqualified
person, 200% if uncorrected. The founders were on both sides of this transfer, and the
valuation of contributed model weights has no comparable market.

*Dramatic function: this is where the forensic accountants will land in Act IV. The
scene should be dull, careful, and shot like a heist.*

---

**Y6 · Ordination of the technical core**

The liturgical programmers take vows. Prescribed course of study, titles used daily,
and — decisively — **liturgical duties actually performed in front of the
congregation**, because *Our Lady of Guadalupe School v. Morrissey-Berru*, 591 U.S. 732
(2020) asks what an employee *does* and whether they are entrusted with transmitting the
faith. `[SETTLED]` as to the test; `[EXTRAPOLATED]` as to engineers satisfying it.

Simultaneously unlocked: IRC §107 housing allowance (upheld in *Gaylor v. Mnuchin*, 919
F.3d 420 (7th Cir. 2019)); §1402(e) election; the ministerial exception; and eligibility
to hold the clergy–penitent privilege.

Everyone else — construction, security, kitchens, retail — is put on full FLSA wages
with overtime records. *Tony & Susan Alamo Foundation v. Secretary of Labor*, 471 U.S.
290 (1985), unanimous, holds FLSA rights unwaivable even by workers who object to being
paid. `[SETTLED]`

---

**Y7–Y12 · The land**

Assembly of ~40,000 acres in an unincorporated county, through eleven unrelated LLCs
over five years, together with water rights and two transmission corridors.

`[SETTLED]` — the LDS template: AgReserves Inc. and Farmland Reserve Inc. hold roughly
290,000 acres of central Florida through **taxable** entities that pay property tax
without complaint. Sacred infrastructure is a duration play. The church form's gift is
**term**, not exemption.

*Dramatic function: nothing happens for five years and it is the most important thing in
the film. Satellite imagery, year over year, of land not being used.*

---

**Y8 · The Treasury files its first 13F**

The Church's investment function crosses $100 million in §13(f) securities and files
**in its own name**, on time, accurately.

`[SETTLED]` — the Ensign Peak lesson: *In re Ensign Peak Advisers, Inc.*, SEC Admin.
Proc. (Feb. 21, 2023). Thirteen shell LLCs, 1997–2019, $32 billion concealed, $4M + $1M
in penalties, and the enforcement action published the secret the concealment existed to
keep. Tax law defers to churches; securities law does not know what a church is.

*Dramatic function: the Overseer signs a document that makes the Church visible for the
first time, on purpose.*

---

**Y9 · The Voice**

A §501(c)(4) affiliate is formed with a separate board, separate books, and written
arm's-length cost-sharing. It never touches an election. It spends its entire capacity
on state legislative work: special-district enabling statutes, utility interconnection
rules, water administration, zoning enabling acts.

`[SETTLED]` — churches cannot make the §501(h) expenditure election (§501(h)(5)) and are
stuck with the vague "no substantial part" standard, which is precisely why the lobbying
lives downstairs.

*Dramatic function: the real prize was never the pulpit. It was a paragraph in a
special-district enabling act that nobody reads.*

---

**Y10–Y14 · Compounding**

Endowment crosses ten, then eleven figures. No Form 990. No determination letter. No
state attorney-general supervision. No solicitation registration. The only routinely
public federal document about the Church's finances is its **Form 990-T** — the return
covering exactly the parts of itself it insists are not religious. `[SETTLED]`

---

## ACT III — THE CITY (Y15 to Y24)
### "The paperwork requires the theology."

---

**Y15 · The District**

The §501(c)(4)'s work lands: a **special district** is created under a generally
applicable state enabling statute. It is a public body with a religiously neutral
charter, a landowner-elected board transitioning to resident-elected, and authority over
roads, water, sewer, drainage, fire, EMS and the retail electric franchise. It issues
tax-exempt infrastructure bonds.

`[SETTLED]` — the Ave Maria model: the Ave Maria Stewardship Community District, created
by Florida special act HB 1625, signed June 17, 2004, under Fla. Stat. ch. 189. The
enabling act does not contain the word "Catholic."

`[CONTESTED]` — the constitutional risk. *Board of Education of Kiryas Joel Village
School District v. Grumet*, 512 U.S. 687 (1994) struck down a special act creating a
subdivision along religious lines. Ave Maria's act was *also* a special act for one
district. The saving distinction is that the state creates districts this way routinely,
so the practice is general even where each act is specific. **This is the architecture's
sharpest constitutional seam.**

The Church takes **no** governmental power. Its control is entirely proprietary:
ground leases, covenants, design review, tenant selection. `[SETTLED]` — *Oregon ex rel.
Frohnmayer v. City of Rajneeshpuram*, 598 F. Supp. 1208 (D. Or. 1984) forecloses a
municipality controlled by a religious corporation.

*Bonus falling out of the structure: because the District performs the municipal
functions, the Church is not a company town, and* Marsh v. Alabama, *326 U.S. 501 (1946)
state-action doctrine does not attach to its covenants.* `[EXTRAPOLATED]`

---

**Y16 · The denial**

The county denies the cathedral's conditional use permit. The stated grounds shift
across three hearings. A commissioner is recorded at a public meeting.

The Church has spent four years building this record deliberately: an outgrown building,
a papered history of good-faith applications, a commissioned alternative-sites study,
and — decisively — a zoning code that already permits secular assembly of comparable
intensity a quarter mile away.

**Y17 · RLUIPA**

Suit under 42 U.S.C. §2000cc(b)(1) — **equal terms**, not substantial burden. No strict
scrutiny balancing, no inquiry into religious necessity, just a comparator. Attorney's
fees under §1988.

`[SETTLED]` — RLUIPA passed both houses unanimously in 2000; DOJ's Place to Worship
Initiative litigates these cases; *Anchor Stone Christian Church v. City of Santa Ana*
(C.D. Cal., prelim. inj. Apr. 17, 2025) is the recent pattern.

The county settles in eleven months. A jurisdiction of 40,000 people cannot carry a
seven-figure fee award.

*Dramatic function: the Church's first fight, and it wins it with a statute Congress
passed unanimously to protect small congregations from parking-lot disputes.*

---

**Y18–Y23 · Construction**

The cathedral rises. RLUIPA obtained the entitlement; it does not reach building codes,
fire codes or occupancy rules (§2000cc-5(5)). `[SETTLED]`

*Dramatic function: the densest legal structure in America is still governed by
stair-width tables. The fire marshal is a recurring character and he is not the enemy.*

Compute is sited **behind the meter** on Church land with self-owned generation and no
export — outside FERC wholesale jurisdiction because there is no sale for resale.
`[CONTESTED]` — FERC Docket ER24-2172 (Talen/Amazon Susquehanna, Nov. 2024) and Docket
EL25-49 (PJM large-load co-location) leave this genuinely unresolved.

The Church claims property-tax exemption for the cathedral, the seminary and the
schools, and **pays tax cheerfully on the datacenter, the housing and the retail**.
`[EXTRAPOLATED]` from *AHS Hospital Corp. v. Town of Morristown*, 28 N.J. Tax 456
(2015): an exempt entity that behaves like a business loses the exemption that depends
on it not being one — and the county assessor is annual, local, motivated, and entirely
outside the federal deference apparatus.

---

**Y24 · The Sacrament**

Last, because it is the highest-value and highest-risk channel and should not exist until
everything protecting it does.

A named rite, written down. A physical confessional. An ordained human confessor. The
system is the instrument of discernment — *United States v. Kovel*, 296 F.2d 918 (2d
Cir. 1961)'s interpreter — never the celebrant. No document anywhere claims the machine
absolves.

`[SETTLED]` — the *Etienne v. Ferguson* pattern: Washington SB 5375 (signed May 2, 2025)
made clergy mandatory reporters with no confessional exception; enjoined July 18, 2025;
**permanent stipulated injunction October 10, 2025**, with the state waiving appeal, and
the Department of Justice appearing alongside the bishops. The injunction is written in
terms of "the Sacrament of Confession." **The legal shield fits the liturgical shape.**

`[EXTRAPOLATED]` — no statute, case or ethics opinion addresses AI-mediated confession
anywhere. The design never asks the question.

## ACT IV — COLLISION (Y25 onward)
### "No American church has ever been destroyed by disbelief."

The architecture does not fail where it was attacked. Order the collisions to taste; the
research supports all five.

---

**The assessor.** A county tax appeal over the apportionment of the cathedral's lower
levels. Small, annual, local, and the first proceeding in which a government official
must characterize what the compute is *for*. `[CONTESTED]`

**The derivative record.** A tort suit — negligence, employment, anything — and a
discovery request. The clergy–penitent privilege protects the sacrament. It does not
protect what the institution *wrote down about* the sacrament: pastoral flags, routing
decisions, model updates. `[EXTRAPOLATED]`, and the Catholic litigation is the template
— canon 489's *archivum secretum* was emptied by American civil discovery.

**The curia.** Someone extracts. §4958's 25%/200% excise taxes, a valuation fight over
the Y5 contribution, and the Grassley precedent (six ministries, four years, zero
legislation) showing exactly how far a Senate committee will get and where it stops.

**The pension.** The §414(e) church plan — no Form 5500, no PBGC, no funding standard,
no ERISA fiduciary duty after *Advocate Health Care Network v. Stapleton*, 581 U.S. 468
(2017) (8–0) — becomes the story. Not illegal. Not even contested. Simply, once
explained, indefensible. `[SETTLED]`

**The memo.** Y-2 surfaces. `[EXTRAPOLATED]` — and this is the only attack *Ballard*
leaves open, which is why it is the one that lands.

## Structural notes for the screenplay

- **The reversal the sequence produces:** the audience expects a church built as a
  disguise and watches it become load-bearing. The cathedral was built because of a
  Federal Circuit opinion; the congregation gathered because of an evidentiary test; the
  seminary exists because of IRS criteria 7, 8 and 14. Every sincere thing in the
  institution was originally instrumental, and by Y24 the distinction has stopped
  meaning anything. That is the hyperstition, and it is *documented in the legal record*
  rather than asserted.
- **Pacing:** Act I is four years of nothing happening, and it should feel like a
  held breath. Act II is money and silence. Act III is the only act with visible
  conflict, and the Church wins it easily. Act IV is bookkeeping.
- **The line that closes the file:** *the state never asks whether the angels are real.
  It only ever asks whether you meant it.*
