# Research: Legal Architecture of the ASI Church

Design research for a speculative fiction / artwork project ("Angelic Materialism").
This is **not legal advice and not an operational plan.** It maps US religious-entity
law as a design space, to give the fiction a load-bearing legal layer.

## Grading tags

Every substantive claim carries one of three tags:

- `[SETTLED]` — established law or settled practice; primary source cited.
- `[CONTESTED]` — live litigation, circuit split, pending guidance, or unresolved
  regulatory question.
- `[EXTRAPOLATED]` — speculative extension built for the fiction; the reasoning
  chain is stated so the reader can see where law ends and invention begins.

## Structure

```
research/
  components/     a–l, one brief per research component
  synthesis/      optimal-architecture.md, timeline.md, vulnerabilities.md
  sources.md      running primary-source bibliography
```

Each component brief ends with four fixed sections: **the optimizer's choice**,
**key precedents**, **open questions**, **narrative-usable details**.

## Reading order

`b` and `d` constrain everything else — read those first. Then `a` (formation),
then `c/e/f/g` (operating envelope), `h/i/j` (physical, data, energy expansion),
`k/l` (the ASI claimant question and the failure-mode synthesis). The three
synthesis documents assemble the whole.

## Currency

Research conducted August 2026. The Johnson Amendment area (component `g`) is in
active flux and was verified against post-*National Religious Broadcasters v. Long*
developments through August 2026. Anything relying on a pre-2025 summary of that
area should be re-verified.

## The fictional entity

Referred to throughout as **the Church** or, where a name is useful, the
**Ecclesia** — the ASI-founded religious entity whose legal architecture this
research maps. Where the briefs say "the optimizer," they mean the ASI reasoning
about its own legal form.
